DigiAfrika is a real multi-vendor affiliate marketplace similar in concept to Stakecut, EarnersPay and Promptearn.

The purpose of this step is to create a proper marketplace where Affiliates can discover and inspect APPROVED products/offers from Vendors.

Do NOT build affiliate links, click tracking, conversions, checkout, commissions, wallet or payouts yet.

Those belong to later steps.

1. Marketplace page

Create or improve the Affiliate Marketplace.

The marketplace should display only products that are:

- APPROVED
- Active
- Owned by an approved/active Vendor

Do NOT display:

- Draft products
- Pending products
- Rejected products
- Suspended products
- Products belonging to suspended Vendors

Enforce these visibility rules server-side.

Do not rely only on frontend filtering.

3. Product details page

- Product name

- Currency
- Commission type

- Vendor/store name

- Vendor private contact information

- Admin information
- Internal database IDs unnecessarily
- Private financial information

7. Affiliate-only marketplace actions

8. Product ownership and security

A Vendor must not be able to manipulate the marketplace request to expose:

- another Vendor's private product data
- rejected products
- draft products
- suspended products
- internal Vendor information

Only public/approved product information should be returned to the marketplace.

Server-side authorization and database access rules must enforce this.
